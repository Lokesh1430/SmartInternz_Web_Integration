About Us

This project integrates four complementary datasets to deliver a comprehensive visualization platform for understanding electric vehicle (EV) performance and charging infrastructure. The vehicle-focused datasets—ElectricCarData_Clean, EVIndia, and Cheapestelectriccars-EVDatabase—provide detailed information on EV specifications such as battery capacity, driving range, charging time, manufacturer, and price. These datasets enable in-depth comparisons across different models, helping users evaluate range efficiency, charging speed, and overall affordability. By cleaning, standardizing, and relating these datasets, the tool ensures consistent and accurate insights into EV capabilities.

The charging infrastructure dataset, electric_vehicle_charging_station_list, adds a geographic dimension by mapping charging stations across India along with charger types (AC and DC Fast). When combined with vehicle performance data, the visualization tool highlights regional charger availability, charging density, and accessibility relative to EV range. Together, all four datasets power interactive dashboards that reveal trends in EV adoption, identify infrastructure gaps, and support data-driven decisions for sustainable mobility planning. This visualization tool ultimately bridges vehicle performance with real-world charging availability, offering a clear and practical view of the evolving electric vehicle ecosystem.
