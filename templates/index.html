About Us

This project integrates four complementary datasets to deliver a comprehensive visualization platform for understanding electric vehicle (EV) performance and charging infrastructure. The vehicle-focused datasets—ElectricCarData_Clean, EVIndia, and Cheapestelectriccars-EVDatabase—provide detailed information on EV specifications such as battery capacity, driving range, charging time, manufacturer, and price.

The charging infrastructure dataset, electric_vehicle_charging_station_list, adds a geographic dimension by mapping charging stations across India along with charger types (AC and DC Fast). When combined with vehicle performance data, the visualization tool highlights regional charger availability, charging density, and accessibility relative to EV range.
